Honest limits: NYC 311 is a city service contact center, not a subscription streaming service; drivers and volumes do not transfer to Netflix. No handle-time (AHT), workload, staffing, or multi-vendor data exists in this dataset, so none is claimed. Event uplifts are measured on a handful of event days and are noisy. The sample of individual contacts covers 3 days (2024-01-08..10) only.
